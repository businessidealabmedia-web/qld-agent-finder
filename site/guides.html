Plain-English help for selling in Queensland.

Checklist

5 questions to ask before you sign with an agent

  1. How many homes have you sold in my suburb this year?
  2. How did you arrive at this price estimate? Can I see comparable sales?
  3. What is your commission and what exactly is included?
  4. What is your marketing plan, and what will it cost me?
  5. Can I speak with some of your recent sellers?
Guide

How to choose the right agent

Look local. An agent who sells regularly in your suburb knows the buyers and the prices.

Don't just chase the highest appraisal. An inflated price can win the listing but leave your home sitting on the market.

Compare results, not promises. Look at recent sales, average days on market and reviews.

Understand the fees. Ask for commission and marketing costs in writing before you sign.

Guide

Getting your home ready to sell

Declutter and deep clean. Fix small repairs. Tidy the garden and entry. Think about styling for photos. Gather documents like rates notices and any building approvals, and speak with a conveyancer early.
