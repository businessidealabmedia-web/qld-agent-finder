Brisbane FAQs

How do I find the best real estate agent in Brisbane?

Look for an agent who sells regularly in your suburb, then compare their recent sales, average days on market, reviews and fees. QLD Agent Finder lets you compare Brisbane agents side by side for free.

How much do real estate agents charge in Brisbane?

Commission varies by agent, property and price, and it is negotiable. Ask each agent for their commission and marketing costs in writing, and use our free commission calculator to compare.
