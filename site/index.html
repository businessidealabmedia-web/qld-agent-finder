Questions sellers ask

Is QLD Agent Finder really free for sellers?

Yes. It is 100% free for home sellers. Agents in our network pay us, never you.

Do I have to list with an agent you match me with?

No. There is no obligation. Meet the agents, compare them and decide in your own time.

How are agents ranked?

Each agent gets a score out of 100 based on reviews, sales volume, speed of sale and experience. See the full method on our rankings page.

Who sees my details?

Only the agents you choose or are matched with. We never sell your information.

Can I just find out what my home is worth?

Yes. Choose “Know my home's value” in the form and a local agent can give you a free home valuation.
