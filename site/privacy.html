Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

QLD Agent Finder ("we", "us") handles personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

Information you give us through our forms, such as your name, phone, email, property address and property details. For agents, we also collect agency, licence and performance details.

Why we collect it

To match sellers with agents, arrange appraisals, display agent profiles, and contact you about your enquiry.

Who we share it with

Seller details are shared only with the agents you choose or are matched with. We never sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and tools such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to understand site use and improve our advertising.

Access, correction and deletion

Email contact@qldagentfinder.com.au at any time.

Complaints

Contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
