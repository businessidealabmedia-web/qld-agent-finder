Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

QLD Agent Finder ("we", "us") is an agent comparison and referral service for Queensland property owners. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

Information you give us through our forms: name, phone, email, property address and details, your timeframe and price expectations, and anything you write in notes or messages. For agents, we also collect agency, licence number, contact details, profile information and performance details such as sales and days on market. We also collect basic website usage data (see Cookies and analytics).

Why we collect it

To match sellers with agents, arrange home valuations, display agent profiles and rankings, respond to enquiries, run and improve our service, and meet our legal obligations. If you are an agent, we also use your details to verify your licence and manage your profile.

Who we share it with

Seller details are shared only with the agents you choose or are matched with, so they can contact you about your enquiry. We never sell personal information. We use trusted service providers to run the site: Supabase (database), Cloudflare (website hosting), Resend (email delivery), and Google and Meta (analytics and advertising measurement). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.

Overseas disclosure

Some of these providers store information outside Australia, including in India. Our team also accesses information from outside Australia, including from Pakistan. By submitting a form you agree to this. We take reasonable steps to make sure overseas recipients protect your information, but they may not be bound by the Australian Privacy Principles.

Marketing

We may contact you about your enquiry and, with your consent, about related services. You can opt out at any time by emailing us.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and tools such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising. You can block cookies in your browser settings, or use the ad settings on Google and Meta to limit tracking.

How long we keep it, and security

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or the law requires. We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect it, but no online system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by law.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Email contact@qldagentfinder.com.au and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

Contact us first at contact@qldagentfinder.com.au. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
