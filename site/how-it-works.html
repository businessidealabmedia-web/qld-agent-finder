How it works

Choosing an agent, made simple

Here is exactly how we help you find the right agent, and how we keep it free.

1

Search or tell us about your home

Search agents by suburb yourself, or fill in our 1-minute form and we will do the matching for you.

2

Compare agents on real results

Every agent profile shows a ranking score, reviews, sales in the last 12 months, average days on market, experience and typical fees. Compare up to 3 side by side.

3

Hear from local agents

Request home valuations from the agents you like. Ask the right questions using our free checklist.

4

Choose with confidence

Pick the agent who fits you best. There is no obligation, and our service is always free for sellers.
